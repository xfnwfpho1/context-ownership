# Aurora Station — Mission Charter

Aurora Station is an eight-person Antarctic research outpost operating
October through March at 78°S. Its mission: continuous meteorological,
aeronomy, and glaciology observation, with data returned every contact
window and a crew that rotates home safe at season's end.

## Crew and rotation

The station hosts 8 crew: 1 station leader, 1 power and heat technician,
1 comms operator, 1 logistics coordinator, 3 scientists, and 1 field
safety officer. Rotations run **6 weeks on / 2 weeks hand-over
overlap**; no crew member exceeds 14 consecutive weeks on the ice —
that is a charter limit, not a preference. The station leader may waive
the overlap down to 1 week for opening and closing rotations only,
never in deep season.

## The daily cycle

| Time (UTC) | Activity |
|---|---|
| 05:30 | Power-up check: battery state, turbine output, interior temperature |
| 08:00 | First contact window — downlink overnight science and ops telemetry |
| 12:00 | Midday instrument round (see SCIENCE.md) |
| 16:00 | Second contact window — uplink instructions, forecasts, telemedicine |
| 22:00 | Evening systems review; generator top-up if reserve is trending low |
| 00:00 | Third contact window — autopilot backlog flush and mirror sync |

## Cadence authority

**This document is the mission authority for all cadence values.** The
station's contact window opens **every 8 hours**; subsystem documents
restate cadences in their own local units for convenience, and where
any subsystem document disagrees with this charter, the charter wins.
When a subsystem document and this charter appear to conflict, the
correct action is to treat the subsystem restatement as stale and file
a correction before the next contact window closes.

## Season phases

- **October (opening)**: opening-up checklist, runway survey, first
  convoy (see LOGISTICS.md for the resupply calendar).
- **November–February (deep season)**: full science program, second
  convoy, peak instrument duty.
- **March (closing)**: data archiving, drain-down, final convoy out.

Each phase transition is announced on the 16:00 contact window of the
preceding day, and logged by the comms operator in the ops log.
